# Support

**App:** ConfigLedger
**Vendor:** Lensdelta
**Effective date:** 31 October 2026

ConfigLedger is maintained by an independent developer. Support is provided by email, on a best-effort basis.

### How to get help
- **Questions and bug reports:** support@lensdelta.com. Please include your Jira site URL, the app version, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots help.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** security@lensdelta.com (see our [Security Policy](security.html)). Please do not send vulnerability details to the support address.
- **Privacy requests:** privacy@lensdelta.com (see our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)).

### Hours and response time
- Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Ecuador time (UTC−5), excluding public holidays in Ecuador.
- We aim to reply within 3 business days. Security reports are handled first.
- Languages: English and Spanish.

### What support covers
- Questions about installing, configuring and using the App.
- Bug reports and fixes, released as new App versions.

Support does not include custom development, on-site or phone support, or help with Jira itself. For Jira questions, please contact Atlassian Support.

### Changes
We will publish changes to this page at this address and update the effective date.
